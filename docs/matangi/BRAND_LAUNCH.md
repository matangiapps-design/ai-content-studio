# MATANGI Faceless Brand Launch

**Brand:** MATANGI  
**Positioning:** Ideas. Intelligence. Influence.  
**Format:** Faceless, text-led or AI-narrated short-form media.  
**Primary channels:** YouTube Shorts, Instagram Reels, Facebook Reels.  
**Initial market:** English-language global audience, with India-friendly examples where useful.

## Editorial promise
Publish useful, entertaining, clearly sourced or clearly framed content about AI, business, science, mysteries, and personal growth. Do not present speculation as fact, fabricated stories as news, or AI scenes as real footage.

## Content pillars
1. AI tools and practical technology.
2. Business, money literacy and entrepreneurship.
3. Science, history and mysteries (verified facts; uncertainty stated).
4. Motivation and personal growth (specific actions, not empty claims).
5. Original cinematic micro-stories and futuristic concepts.

## 30-day production target
- 2 original vertical videos per day.
- 60 source videos, adapted to each platform's native requirements.
- YouTube Shorts, Instagram Reels and Facebook Reels: up to 180 platform posts.
- One weekly YouTube compilation only when the source videos form a coherent, genuinely useful episode.

This is a test cadence, not a promise of reach or revenue. Reduce volume if quality, accuracy, or audience retention drops.

## Video specification
- 9:16, 1080x1920 master; export platform-specific versions.
- 20–45 seconds for most short-form posts.
- Hook in first 1–2 seconds; one core idea; clear payoff; concise CTA.
- Burned-in captions, high contrast, safe margins, licensed/original visuals and audio.
- No borrowed creator clips, unlicensed music, fake testimonials, fake earnings claims, or engagement bait.

## Weekly operating loop
1. Research and shortlist 14 ideas.
2. Verify factual claims and record sources in the content log.
3. Draft scripts and visual shot lists.
4. Generate or create visuals; review every scene.
5. Render, caption, check audio and export.
6. Schedule natively/through an approved scheduler after accounts are connected.
7. Review retention, shares, saves, profile visits, clicks and sales; repeat only winning topics.

## Monetisation sequence
1. Validate audience demand before building a large catalogue.
2. Free lead magnet: "AI Creator Starter Checklist".
3. Entry product: original prompt/workflow toolkit, suggested test price US$9.
4. Bundle: creator workflow templates and short-form content planner, suggested test price US$19–29.
5. Later: monthly membership or done-for-you content service only after demand and delivery capacity are proven.

Prices are experiments, not forecasts. Display clear product contents, refund terms, contact details and privacy terms. Do not promise income or guaranteed outcomes.

## Funnel
Short video -> profile link -> landing page -> free checklist/email opt-in -> useful follow-up sequence -> paid toolkit -> customer support/refund handling.

Track UTM-tagged links and weekly conversion rates. Collect only necessary customer data and obtain consent for marketing email.

## Platform integrity
- Disclose realistic synthetic/altered content wherever platform rules require it.
- Do not impersonate real people or fabricate real-world events.
- Use original scripts and visuals, licensed assets, and platform-approved music.
- Check current monetisation and originality rules before applying to any partner programme.
- Never auto-publish until the account connection, permissions, preview and post metadata are verified.

## Go-live gates
- Social accounts connected and correct brand selected.
- Brand profile, avatar, bio and landing page reviewed.
- Product checkout tested end-to-end, including refund/contact path.
- At least 10 finished videos quality-checked.
- Analytics and link tracking working.
- A human approves the first week of posts.
