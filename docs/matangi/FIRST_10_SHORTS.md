# MATANGI — First 10 Short-Video Scripts

All scripts are original starting drafts. Verify any factual claim and customize visuals before publication. Target length: 20–35 seconds. Voice-free version: use kinetic captions and sound design; optional narration may be synthetic and clearly disclosed when required.

## 01 — Your first AI workflow
**Pillar:** AI tools  
**Hook:** "Stop asking AI random questions. Give it a job."  
**Script:** "Choose one repeated task. Tell AI the role, the goal, the audience, the constraints, and the exact output format. Then ask it to check its own work against your rules. Save the prompt. Improve it after every use. That's a workflow—not a magic button."  
**Visuals:** Fast sequence: messy notes -> structured prompt -> clean checklist.  
**CTA:** "Follow MATANGI for practical AI workflows."  
**Caption:** A repeatable workflow beats random prompting.  
**Tags:** #AITools #AIWorkflow #MATANGI

## 02 — The hidden cost of a cheap product
**Pillar:** Business  
**Hook:** "A cheap product can become your most expensive mistake."  
**Script:** "Price is only one part of cost. Add shipping, returns, support time, packaging, payment fees and unsold stock. Then calculate what remains per sale. If the margin disappears after the real costs, it isn't a bargain for your business."  
**Visuals:** Price tag expands into a cost breakdown.  
**CTA:** "Save this before your next purchase."  
**Caption:** Calculate total cost, not just the sticker price.  
**Tags:** #SmallBusiness #BusinessTips #MATANGI

## 04 — Why the sky changes colour
**Pillar:** Science  
**Hook:** "Why can a sunset look red or orange?"  
**Script:** "Sunlight contains many colours. When the Sun is low, its light travels through more atmosphere before reaching your eyes. Shorter blue wavelengths are scattered out of the direct path more strongly, leaving warmer colours more visible. The atmosphere is doing the filtering."  
**Visuals:** Simple sunlight ray diagram; sunset time-lapse from licensed footage.  
**CTA:** "Follow for science made simple."  
**Caption:** A beautiful effect of light scattering.  
**Tags:** #ScienceExplained #Nature #MATANGI

## 06 — A simple scam warning
**Pillar:** Digital literacy  
**Hook:** "A message can look official and still be fake."  
**Script:** "Pause before clicking. Check the sender independently. Don't share one-time passwords or recovery codes. Open the official app or type the known website address yourself. Urgency is a common pressure tactic. Verify first."  
**Visuals:** Generic fake-message mock-up with all personal details fictional.  
**CTA:** "Share this digital safety reminder."  
**Caption:** Never share OTPs or recovery codes.  
**Tags:** #CyberSafety #DigitalLiteracy #MATANGI

## 07 — The sunk-cost trap
**Pillar:** Business thinking  
**Hook:** "You don't have to keep funding a bad decision."  
**Script:** "Money already spent cannot be recovered just because you spend more. Ask what the next rupee will realistically produce. Compare the future options—not the emotional cost of stopping. Learn from the past, decide for the future."  
**Visuals:** Road fork; sunk-cost coins fade; decision checklist appears.  
**CTA:** "Save this decision rule."  
**Caption:** Make the next decision on future value.  
**Tags:** #BusinessMindset #DecisionMaking #MATANGI

## 08 — Mystery versus evidence
**Pillar:** Mystery/science  
**Hook:** "A mystery is not proof of the supernatural."  
**Script:** "An unexplained event means the available evidence hasn't settled the question. It does not automatically prove the most dramatic explanation. Separate what was observed, what was claimed, and what can actually be tested. Curiosity and skepticism belong together."  
**Visuals:** Dark archive, evidence cards, magnifying glass, source labels.  
**CTA:** "Follow for mysteries examined carefully."  
**Caption:** Stay curious. Check the evidence.  
**Tags:** #Mystery #CriticalThinking #MATANGI

## 10 — The 24-hour idea filter
**Pillar:** Productivity  
**Hook:** "Before building an idea, test it in one day."  
**Script:** "Write down the customer, the problem, and the smallest result you can deliver. Show the offer to five real potential customers. Ask what they already use, what frustrates them, and whether they would pay for your solution. Evidence first. Building second."  
**Visuals:** Idea note -> five conversations -> simple offer page.  
**CTA:** "Test demand before spending."  
**Caption:** Validate the problem before building the product.  
**Tags:** #Entrepreneurship #StartupLessons #MATANGI

## Production checklist for every post
- Original script and rights-cleared assets.
- Strong first frame and readable captions.
- No unsupported factual, medical, financial or earnings claims.
- Correct AI/synthetic disclosure where required.
- Correct title, caption, thumbnail/cover and platform settings.
- Human preview before scheduling.
- Record post URL and performance metrics after publishing.
