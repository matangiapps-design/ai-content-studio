# MATANGI — First Product & Sales Funnel

## Lead magnet
**Name:** AI Creator Starter Checklist  
**Promise:** A practical one-page checklist to plan, produce, review and repurpose an original short video.  
**Contents:** idea selection, hook, script, shot list, rights check, caption check, disclosure check, export and analytics.

## Entry offer (test, not a guarantee)
**Product:** MATANGI Short-Form Creator Toolkit  
**Suggested launch price:** US$9 introductory / localized equivalent shown at checkout.

Deliverables:
- 30 original hook frameworks (not copied scripts).
- 10 short-video script templates.
- 7-day content calendar.
- Shot-list and caption worksheets.
- Pre-publish rights, accuracy and disclosure checklist.
- Simple weekly performance tracker.

## Bundle (only after validating the entry offer)
**Product:** MATANGI Creator Workflow Bundle  
**Suggested test price:** US$19–29.
Includes the entry toolkit plus a 30-day planner, repurposing matrix and prompt workflow library.

## Funnel steps
1. Profile link -> landing page.
2. Landing page -> free checklist email opt-in.
3. Welcome email -> checklist delivery and one practical tip.
4. Follow-up email -> show toolkit contents and sample pages.
5. Checkout -> clear price, deliverables, refund terms and support contact.
6. Post-purchase -> delivery email, support path and optional feedback request.

## Validation before building checkout
- Interview or survey at least 10 target creators.
- Publish 10 videos and measure profile visits and link clicks.
- Ask what specific workflow problem they want solved.
- Do not claim demand from views alone.
- Build only the promised deliverables; test download and payment on mobile.
- Publish privacy, refund, terms and contact information before taking payment.

## Metrics
Views -> engaged views/retention -> profile visits -> link clicks -> opt-ins -> checkout starts -> purchases -> refunds.
Track by channel with UTM tags. Review weekly; do not use follower count as the only success metric.
