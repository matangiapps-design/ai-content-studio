# ODIA HERO — Scene Prompts & Odia Voiceover Pack

Production target: three films, each exactly 04:04 (244 seconds), 16:9. Each film uses 12 shots: eleven 20-second slots and a final 24-second slot. Generate each shot as a separate Wan 3.0 clip, then trim/assemble to the exact timecodes. The narration below is a recording script; adjust pauses and delivery during the edit.

## Global visual bible (apply to every shot)

**Biku identity lock:** small white stickman hero; round white head; expressive black oval eyes; thin black arms and legs; bright red superhero cape. Keep identical head size, body proportions, eye placement, cape shape and colours in every shot. Do not add clothing, hair, logos or extra accessories.

**World/style:** polished cinematic 3D family animation; Puri, Odisha during Jagannath Rath Yatra; respectful devotional atmosphere; authentic colourful decorated Rath, flags and festival details; stylised animated stickman people only; expressive but gentle acting; natural motion; soft cinematic lighting; clear staging; no live-action people; no on-screen text; no watermark. 16:9 landscape.

**Negative prompt:** character redesign, changed cape, missing cape, extra limbs, malformed hands, distorted faces, flicker, inconsistent scale, duplicated characters, unreadable signs, watermark, logo, live-action people, disrespectful depiction of Jagannath or the Rath, dangerous crowd movement, children near moving wheels, anyone touching or trying to move the Rath.

**Audio:** record the Odia voiceover separately. Use original or properly licensed devotional instrumental music. Do not generate dialogue in the video model; add narration, ambience and subtitles in post.

## Recording and edit notes

1. Have a native Odia speaker review pronunciation, grammar and local phrasing before final recording.
2. Record each narration row with a natural pause; the table rows are scene cues, not a requirement to speak continuously for the entire shot.
3. Keep music below narration. Add subtle rain, crowd, conch and bell ambience only where appropriate and properly licensed.
4. Create Odia subtitles from the approved final voice recording, not from an unreviewed draft.
5. Keep the final film duration exactly 244 seconds after trimming. Do not stretch shots unnaturally to fill time.
6. Maintain the same Biku reference image and character description for every generation. If the provider supports reference-image conditioning, attach the same approved Biku reference to every scene.
7. These are production-ready creative briefs, not rendered footage. Review all clips for cultural respect, character continuity, child safety and technical quality before publishing.
