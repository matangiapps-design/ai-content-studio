# ODIA HERO — Biku: The Little Hero of Rath Yatra

### Scene 2 (00:20–00:40)

### Scene 3 (00:40–01:00)

### Scene 4 (01:00–01:20)

### Scene 6 (01:40–02:00)

### Scene 7 (02:00–02:20)

### Scene 8 (02:20–02:40)
